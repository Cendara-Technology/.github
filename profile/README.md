## 🏗️ Cendara Foundation Framework™

Our methodology provides a practical path for building technology foundations that endure.

```text
Discover
   ↓
Align
   ↓
Design
   ↓
Deliver
   ↓
Operate
   ↓
Evolve
```

### 01 — Discover

Understand the organisation, stakeholders, services, risks, constraints and existing technology foundations.

### 02 — Align

Connect technology priorities with business outcomes, governance and investment intent.

### 03 — Design

Define practical target-state architecture, controls, operating models and transition pathways.

### 04 — Deliver

Execute change through disciplined planning, technical coordination and controlled implementation.

### 05 — Operate

Embed support, monitoring, lifecycle management, security and governance into everyday operations.

### 06 — Evolve

Measure outcomes, improve maturity and continuously evolve technology foundations as the organisation changes.
